## 1. UMUMIY USLUB (har 5 ta promptda bir xil)

### Kompozitsiya:
```
- Stadium markazda, frame'ning ~50-60% qismini egallaydi
- Wide landscape orientation (16:9)
- Generous space atrofida (floating elements uchun)
- Stadium butunligicha ko'rinadi (kesilmagan)
- Cinematic 3/4 angle yoki orbital view
```

### Background (har 5 ta rasmda bir xil):
```
Deep cosmic background — dark navy black (#0A0E1A) with subtle 
starfield, lunar/desert rocky landscape at the bottom horizon, 
atmospheric haze, floating dust particles, distant planets or 
moons visible, sense of floating in space, otherworldly mood.
```

### Floating elements (har card uchun farqli, lekin uslub bir xil):
```
Various objects floating around the central stadium with subtle 
motion blur on edges, scattered at different distances creating 
depth, items related to the stadium's character (lights, banners, 
microphones, fireworks, etc.).
```

### Kamera va sifat (bir xil):
```
Shot with cinematic anamorphic lens at moderate distance, f/4.0 
medium depth of field, cinematic color grading by Roger Deakins, 
8K resolution, photorealistic, hyperdetailed, no CGI plastic look, 
no cartoon, no illustration, atmospheric lighting.
```

## 4. CONSISTENCY UCHUN (eng muhim)

### Strategiya: STADIUM 1 (METLIFE) ni reference qiling

```
1. Stadium 1 (MetLife) ni avval generatsiya qiling — eng yaxshi sifatga
2. URL'ini saqlang
3. Boshqa 4 ta stadium'ni Stadium 1 ni reference qilib chiqaring:

   [Stadium X prompt] --sref [STADIUM 1 URL] --sw 100 --ar 16:9 --seed 44444
```

## 6. MASLAHATLAR

### Agar stadium butunligicha chiqmasa (kesilgan):
- Promptga qo'shing: `entire stadium visible, full structure in frame, no cropping, wide shot showing complete venue`
- Negative'ga: `cropped stadium, partial stadium, cut-off architecture`

### Agar background'da osmon ko'k chiqsa:
- Promptga: `nighttime cosmic black sky, deep navy void, NOT daytime, NOT blue sky`
- Negative'ga: `daytime, blue sky, sunny weather, white clouds`

### Agar floating elements yo'q yoki kam chiqsa:
- Har card'da `Floating elements` ro'yxatini **takror** ta'kidlang
- `multiple objects floating in space around the stadium` qo'shing
- `dynamic composition with scattered elements` qo'shing

### Agar har rasmda atmosfera o'zgarib ketsa:
- **Eng muhim:** `--sref [Stadium 1 URL]` ni MAJBURIY ishlatish
- `--sw 100` (full style weight)
- Promptda background qatorini **so'zma-so'z bir xil** yozish
