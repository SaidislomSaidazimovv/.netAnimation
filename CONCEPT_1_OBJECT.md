## 5. TOP 3 TAVSIYA

### 🥇 VARIANT 1: Butterfly Sign Hands Monument

**Tasvir:**
- Bronza yoki marmar haykal, **3-4 metr balandlik**
- Ikki qo'l birga, boshmaldoqlar kesishgan, barmoqlar qanot kabi yoyilgan
- Galereya/muzey kabi yoritilgan, professional spotlight

**Mato ostida:** Tik, vertikal, sirli shakl — go'yo katta yodgorlik

**Ochilganda:** **"BU O'SHA BELGI"** — darhol tushuniladi

**Nima uchun eng kuchli:**
- Bu **brendning o'zi** — "first universal gesture"
- Cinematic — yangi yodgorlik tantanali ochilishi kabi
- Eternal/sacred his qildiradi
- Ford "mashina" obyektining mukammal ekvivalenti
- "Butterfly Sign" matni shart emas — ko'rinish o'zi gapiradi

```
Monumental bronze sculpture of two hands forming a butterfly symbol, 
thumbs crossed, fingers spread like wings, 3 meters tall, displayed 
in a dark gallery with single overhead spotlight, museum lighting, 
dramatic shadows, photorealistic, shot on Hasselblad medium format, 
shallow depth of field, polished bronze surface with golden 
highlights, dark stone pedestal
```

## 7. KEYINGI QADAMLAR (Variant tasdiqlangach)

### Image generation (siz qilasiz):

1. **8-10 ta keyframe rasm** generatsiya (Midjourney, Flux, Sora image mode)
2. Har bir kadr — animatsiyaning bir pozitsiyasi:
   - Kadr 1: Wide shot, to'liq mato
   - Kadr 2: Kamera 15° aylangan, mato shamol bilan tebranadi
   - Kadr 3: Kamera 30°, mato yuqoridan ochila boshlaydi
   - Kadr 4: Kamera 45°, mato yarim ochiq
   - Kadr 5: Kamera 60°, qo'l/figura ko'rinadi
   - Kadr 6: Kamera 75°, mato tushib bormoqda
   - Kadr 7: Kamera 90° (yon), obyekt to'liq ochilgan
   - Kadr 8: Final, atrof yorug'lik bilan

3. Eng yaxshilarini tanlash → menga yuborasiz
4. Men ularni Runway "Image to Video" rejimida bog'layman (kadrlar orasi smooth tranzitsiya)
5. FFmpeg bilan birlashtirib, 240 ta kadrga ajrataman
6. Saytga integratsiya
