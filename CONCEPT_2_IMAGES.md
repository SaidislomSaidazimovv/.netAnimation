## 2. 5 TA KEYFRAME — TO'LIQ PROMPTLAR

### ⚠️ KOMPOZITSIYA QOIDASI (5 RASMDA HAM)

**MUHIM:** Sayyora saytlari (spaceedu) kabi — **obyekt to'liq ko'rinishi shart**, hech bir tomondan kesilmagan bo'lishi kerak. Obyekt va u joylashgan kontekst (yaproq/novda) **butun frame ichida** ko'rinishi kerak. Atrofda **bo'sh joy** (negative space) bo'lsin.

```
Composition rule for ALL frames:
- Subject FULLY VISIBLE — no cropping on any edge
- Subject and its surrounding context (leaf/twig) ENTIRELY within frame
- Camera positioned at moderate distance — NOT extreme close-up
- Generous negative space on all sides (top, bottom, left, right)
- Object centered with breathing room around it
- Like a planet floating in space — complete and contained
```
